## Instructions


### 1. Prep
Preheat your oven to **375°F (190°C)**. Lightly grease your baking dish with a little margarine or cooking spray.


### 2. Cook the Pasta
Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the **12 oz of rigatoni** and cook according to the package directions until **al dente** (usually 10–12 minutes). Drain and set aside.


> **Tip:** Al dente means the pasta is still slightly firm when bitten — it will finish cooking in the oven.


### 3. Cook the Chicken
While the pasta cooks, cut the **1.5 lbs of chicken breast** into bite-sized cubes (about 1 inch). Heat **2 tablespoons of margarine** in a large skillet over medium-high heat.


Add the chicken cubes and season with:
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
- 1 teaspoon garlic powder
- 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning


Cook for **6–8 minutes**, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is browned on the outside and cooked through (no pink in the center). Remove from heat.


### 4. Make the Sauce
In a large bowl, combine:
- The **24 oz jar of tomato pasta sauce**
- **1/2 cup of fat-free milk**
- **2 oz of grated Parmesan** (about 1/2 cup)


Stir until smooth. The milk helps make the sauce creamier and stretches it to coat everything nicely.


### 5. Assemble the Bake
In your greased baking dish, combine:
- Cooked rigatoni
- Cooked chicken
- The sauce mixture


Toss everything together until evenly coated. Spread it out in an even layer.


### 6. Top with Cheese
Sprinkle the **8 oz of shredded mozzarella** evenly over the top, then add the remaining **2 oz of grated Parmesan**.


### 7. Bake
Place the dish in the preheated oven and bake for **20–25 minutes**, until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and starting to turn golden brown around the edges.


### 8. Rest and Serve
Let the pasta bake cool for **5 minutes** before serving — this helps it set and makes portioning easier.

## Beginner Tips


- **Don't overcook the pasta** in step 2 — it will keep cooking in the oven.
- **Cut chicken evenly** so it all cooks at the same rate.
- **Let it rest** after baking so the sauce thickens and you get clean slices.
- **No 9×13 dish?** Use two smaller dishes or a deep oven-safe skillet.
